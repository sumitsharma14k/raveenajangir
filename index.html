About Me

Hello! I'm Raveena Jangir, a passionate fashion designer specializing in ethnic wear. With a deep appreciation for traditional Indian craftsmanship and a modern design sensibility, I create stunning pieces that celebrate the rich heritage of Indian textiles while appealing to contemporary tastes. Each design tells a story of culture, elegance, and timeless beauty.
